### Top 3 Useful Info Items You Should Add

Adding these 3 metrics will make your dashboard look like a **professional institutional screener**:

#### 1. Today's Price % Change (`PRICE CHANGE %`)
* **Why:** High volume alone doesn't tell the whole story.
  * **Volume Spike + Price UP (+5%)** = 🟢 **Aggressive Institutional Buying / Breakout**
  * **Volume Spike + Price DOWN (-5%)** = 🔴 **Institutional Dumping / Panic Selling**

#### 2. VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price) — *Replaces Fake Buy/Sell Zones*
* **Why:** VWAP is the exact benchmark institutional traders use. 
  * If **Price > VWAP**: Buyers are in control (Bullish).
  * If **Price < VWAP**: Sellers are in control (Bearish).

#### 3. RVOL (Relative Volume Multiplier)
* **Why:** Instead of showing `+250%`, showing **`3.5x Normal`** is much faster for a trader to read instantly.
